This item sets **every** frontmatter field. `repo` and `demo` add the buttons under the title, `tech` becomes the tags, `category` groups it in the list, and `date` sorts the list newest first. For a portfolio, each item would be one project.

The island's **HOVER ME** button opens this item directly (`contentItemSlug`) and shows its hover card (`hoverCardItem: "list/sample-item"`).
