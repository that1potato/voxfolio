This is a **panel** section: one MDX file, `content/article.mdx`, opened by the island's _ARTICLE PANEL_ button. Its heading comes from the `title` in the frontmatter. For a personal site this is where your "About me" would go. Rename the section in `site.config.ts` and point a button at it in `/editor`.

## Markdown, the usual way

- **Bold**, _italic_, `inline code` and [links](https://example.com)
- Bullet lists like this one
- Tables, task lists and strikethrough (GFM)

1. Numbered lists work too
2. So do headings from `##` down

| You write          | Readers see                   |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------- |
| `content/*.mdx`    | A frosted-glass article panel |
| `content/<id>/*`   | A list of cards, like LIST PANEL |
| `public/…`         | Images, GIFs and videos       |
